Who we are

An Omani company.. with a GCC vision

Established in 2023, Al Saaeid For Business Joint Partnership is a food and beverage manufacturing company based in Fanja, Sultanate of Oman.

We specialize in the production of premium beverages, dairy products and ready-to-eat food products, combining traditional flavors with modern manufacturing practices. Our commitment is to provide consumers with nutritious, hygienic, high-quality products that meet the evolving needs of families, retailers, restaurants and food service businesses across Oman and the GCC region.
